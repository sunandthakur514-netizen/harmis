## 1. Who may use HARMIS

HARMIS is a personal tool. It is used **only by its owner, Anand**, to make
original animated Hindi story videos for children and to upload them to the

use it to access their accounts.

## 2. What HARMIS does

- It makes videos from original scripts and public-domain folk tales
  (Panchatantra, Jataka, Akbar-Birbal, Tenali Raman), with pictures, voices and
  subtitles made by free tools.
- It uploads those videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube
  Data API with **only** the `youtube.upload` permission, and posts short
  videos to the owner's own Instagram account through the Instagram Graph API.
- It does not access, collect or change any other person's data or accounts.

## 3. Content rules
